# Acme Bakery: customer FAQ

## Opening hours
The shop is open Monday to Saturday from 7:00 to 19:00, and on Sundays from 8:00 to 14:00. We are
closed on Diwali and on New Year's Day.

## Delivery
We deliver within 8 km of the Indiranagar shop in Bengaluru. Orders placed before 11:00 are delivered
the same day. The delivery fee is 60 rupees, and delivery is free for orders above 800 rupees.

## Custom cakes
Custom cakes need 3 days' notice. We take a 50% deposit when the order is confirmed. If a customer
cancels at least 48 hours before pickup, the deposit is refunded.

## Allergens
Everything is made in one kitchen that also handles nuts, gluten, milk and eggs, so we cannot guarantee
that any product is nut-free. Our eggless range is clearly labelled on the shelf and on the website.

## Refunds and replacements
If a product arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, send us a photo within 24 hours of delivery
and we will refund or replace it.
