# Acme Bakery: privacy policy

## What we collect
For the newsletter we collect an email address and nothing else. For orders we collect a name, a
delivery address and a phone number, so we can deliver and contact the customer about the order.

## Why we collect it
Newsletter emails are used only to send our weekly offers. Order details are used only to prepare and
deliver the order.

## How long we keep it
Newsletter email addresses are kept for 24 months after the last activity, or until the subscriber
unsubscribes, whichever is sooner. Order records are kept for 7 years because tax rules require it.

## Who we share it with
We never sell personal data. We use two processors: an email service provider for the newsletter
(Mailchimp is planned) and Razorpay for payments.

## Your rights
Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link that works immediately. To ask for your data to be
deleted, write to privacy@acmebakery.example and we will respond within 30 days.

## Consent
People join the newsletter by submitting the sign-up form themselves. We do not pre-tick any box.
Double opt-in (a confirmation email) is not required today, but the marketing lead is reviewing it.
