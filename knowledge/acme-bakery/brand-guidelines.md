# Acme Bakery: brand guidelines

## Colours
- Warm Crust, #C8873A: primary buttons and highlights.
- Flour White, #FBF7F0: page background.
- Rye Brown, #4A2F1B: body text.
- Error Red, #B3261E: error messages only.

## Typography
Headings use Fraunces. Body text uses Inter at 16 pixels or larger.

## Tone of voice
Friendly and short. Error messages never use exclamation marks and never blame the customer. A success
message may use one exclamation mark.

## Logo
Keep 16 pixels of clear space around the logo. Never place it on a photograph.

## Accessibility
Text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background. Buttons and links must be at
least 44 pixels high so they are easy to tap on a phone.
