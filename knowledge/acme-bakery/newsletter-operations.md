# Acme Bakery: newsletter operations

## Schedule
The weekly offers email is sent on Fridays at 09:00 India time.

## Ownership
Priya Nair, the marketing lead, owns the subscriber list and approves every newsletter before it goes
out. Nobody else may export the list.

## Tools
We plan to use Mailchimp for sending. Until then the list is exported from the sign-up database as a
CSV file once a week.

## Targets
The goal for the first year is 500 subscribers. We aim for an average open rate of 35%.

## Not planned
SMS and WhatsApp campaigns are not planned this year.
